## 9. Initramfs observation contract

1. `CMDLINE_PARSED`
2. `ROOT_DEVICE_RESOLVED`
3. `USERDATA_PROBED`
4. `ROOTFS_MOUNTED`
5. `DEV_MOVE_BEGIN` / `DEV_MOVE_DONE`
6. `CONSOLE_OPEN_OK` or `CONSOLE_OPEN_FAILED`
7. `RUN_MOVE_BEGIN` / `RUN_MOVE_DONE`
8. `HANDOFF_MARKER_VISIBLE` or `HANDOFF_MARKER_MISSING`
9. `RUN_INIT_EXEC`

`USERDATA_PROBED` must precede `ROOTFS_MOUNTED`.
